**ClaimCheck** does the first pass of mass tort claimant qualification. It reads each claimant's records, extracts
the facts that matter with verifiable citations, checks them against the case criteria, and sends anything uncertain
to a person.

**How a claimant moves through it**

1. **Intake:** matters and documents are pulled from the firm's case management system (mock), uploaded through the
   API, or dropped into a watched folder. Scanned pages are OCR'd.
2. **Extraction:** a free pattern extractor reads known layouts; a local LLM handles the pages it can't (hybrid mode).
3. **Verification:** each fact must quote the record, and code checks that the quote and its dates and codes
   are really on the cited page. Unverified facts never count as proof.
4. **Criteria:** deterministic rules from the case's YAML file decide *qualified / not qualified / needs review*,
   with the evidence behind each criterion.
5. **Agent:** for files that need review, the Case Review Agent searches the records, flags what to check, and
   drafts records requests. It can't decide anything or send anything.
6. **People:** paralegals approve or reject facts; attorneys sign off on claimants (overrides need a written reason)
   and approve records requests. Everything is in the audit log, and results sync back to the CMS.

**Roles in this demo:** switch the user in the sidebar. `a.rivera` is an attorney; `j.chen` is a paralegal and
can't record decisions, approve requests, sync the CMS, or read the full audit log.

**Data:** all claimants, records and the litigation itself are fictional and generated by `claimcheck/synth.py`.
